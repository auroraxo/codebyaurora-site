Is "your committed Anchor IDL no longer matches what is deployed" a topic about live programs people still use, or about archives nobody touches? I measured it. These are the three numbers that answer it — each with the raw artifact that produced it.

Honest limits

  • 29.61% is a coverage statement, not a bug count: a closed ProgramData means the on-chain IDL copy is gone, not that the program is broken.
  • 87.53% is a sample of 393 decided programs (CI95 83.90–90.44), not the full population. "Alive" = successful transaction after last deploy, inside the node's signature retention window — rarely-used programs can look dead.
  • Upstream reactions are a small sample: one merged and released, the rest still open without maintainer reply. Both facts are stated, not smoothed into a story.
  • The niche is drawing independent builders — a 2★ file-diff tool and a 0★ CI gate appeared next to my own on-chain IDL drift action. That is demand for the topic, not for my tool.
